⭕ Overhead distance

How close a plane has to pass to set off the overhead alerts: the banner, the full-screen close-up and the voice. It's the red dashed circle around home on the map, and changes as you drag.

🗣 Announcement voice

A voice speaks when a plane passes inside the overhead circle.

Click to add:

Anything the app doesn't know for a plane (like where it's going) is left out.

Kokoro voices sound the most natural and are made right on this PC: the first one you pick downloads once (about 90 to 300 MB) and the browser keeps it. The browser's own voices are instant; Edge's "Natural" ones only work when Microsoft's speech service can be reached. Each browser remembers its own choice.

To turn the alerts on or off, use Overhead Plane Alert and 🔔 Sound at the top of the page.

🔍 Size of everything

Make text, buttons and panels bigger or smaller. Handy on a TV or wall screen. Changes right away.

📏 Units

Off: miles per hour and miles. Altitudes are always in feet. The voice uses the same units.

🪟 Dashboard window

Off: the dashboard opens in a normal browser tab, with Edge's natural, upbeat voice. Sound needs one click on the page after it opens. On: it opens in its own window (Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome, without the address bar) where the chime and voice work straight away, but only with Windows' plainer built-in voice (Zira). Used when the app starts and when you click the tray icon.

⬆ Updates

🚢 Ship tracking optional, free

Shows cruise ships and other boats near you, live, using AISStream.io. It's free, but you need your own key:

  1. Go to aisstream.io and click Sign in (with your GitHub account).
  2. Open API Keys and click Create.
  3. Copy the key and paste it below, then click Save.

Your key stays on this PC (in settings.json). AISStream receives the area around your home so it can send the ships there.

The app remembers where these lines' ships were last seen anywhere in the world, so you can jump to them by name. AISStream can watch up to 190 ships, so choosing fewer lines leaves room for all of their ships.

📡 FlightAware feeder optional

Only if you run your own ADS-B receiver that feeds FlightAware. Your stats then show in the right-hand panel.

Advanced: photo lookup contact
